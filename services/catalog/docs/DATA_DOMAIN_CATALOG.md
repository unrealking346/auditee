# Waeve Data Domain Catalog

The database now models the full product surface rather than only playback.

## Identity & privacy
users, profiles, devices, sessions, passkeys, social identities, verification tokens, password resets, consent, exports and deletion requests.

## Catalog & media
artists, albums, tracks, versions, releases, credits, genres, moods, tags, media assets, fingerprints, waveforms, lyrics, lyric corrections, booklets and album notes.

## Rights & integrity
rights, rights history, takedowns, UGC claims, catalog-quality issues and audit records.

## Listening
history, sessions, events, queues, playback preferences, downloads, smart downloads, mixes, radio stations, transitions and device handoffs.

## Discovery
recommendation profiles, impressions, experiments, search telemetry, charts, editorial and editorial collections.

## Social
playlists, collaborators, comments, reactions, listening rooms, communities, posts, follows, notifications and share links.

## Artist economy
artist teams, verification, releases, submissions, fan support, storefronts, merch, events, royalty splits, rates, statements and payouts.

## Commerce
plans, offers, subscriptions, payment methods and billing event reconciliation.

## Adjacent media
podcasts, episodes, progress, music videos, live events and short clips.

## Global operations
regional configuration, localization, campaigns, sponsorships, support tickets, incident announcements, daily service metrics, moderation and fraud signals.
